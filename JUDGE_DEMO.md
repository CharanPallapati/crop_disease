
# 3-minute judge demo

## 0:00 — Problem
"Farmers don't need another diagnosis screen. They need to know what is wrong, what to do, and whether the next few hours are safe to act."

## 0:30 — Input
Upload a real field leaf image and allow location access.

## 0:55 — AI diagnosis
Show:
- crop
- disease
- confidence
- top alternatives
- image quality gate

Explain that low-confidence images are rejected instead of producing a confident hallucination.

## 1:20 — Climate bridge
Show live hourly forecast:
- rain probability
- humidity
- temperature
- wind

Then show ACT / CAUTION / WAIT.

## 1:50 — Action window
Show the next forecast window that meets the application's weather criteria.

## 2:10 — Trust
Show the agronomic guidance and source cards.
Explain that the recommendation layer is designed around IPM and local validation rather than inventing pesticide doses.

## 2:30 — Farmer access
Press Telugu + Speak.
Show the same decision in farmer-friendly Telugu.

## 2:45 — Differentiator
"Most crop AI stops at classification. AgriShield closes the loop from field evidence to weather-aware timing."

## 3:00 — Impact
"Earlier detection + better timing + less unnecessary chemical use = better livelihood resilience and food security."
